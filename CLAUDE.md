# Instructions for Claude in this repository

**Read `brand/BRAND.md` before producing or editing any Rockit asset.** It records permanent decisions from the brand owner. These two are non-negotiable:

1. **Gold is always the metallic gradient from the Rockit logo.** Never use a flat yellow.
2. **The Rockit logo is on every branded asset.** Use `brand/rockit-logo.png` on light backgrounds and `brand/rockit-logo-reversed.png` on dark ones.

The weekly newsletter (The Rockit Manifest) lives in `newsletter/`. Follow `newsletter/RUNBOOK.md`. Its builder, `newsletter/tools/build_issue.py`, already encodes the brand rules: the gold tokens `GOLD_GRAD`, `GT`, `svg_gold`, and the logo copied into every issue's `visuals/`. Do not reintroduce `#d5ae69` or any other flat gold.
