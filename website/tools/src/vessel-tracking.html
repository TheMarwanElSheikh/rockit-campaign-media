Live AIS map centred on the Suez Canal, Mediterranean and Red Sea approaches.Only know the ship's name? Look up its IMO on VesselFinder, then paste the number here.

Positions come from the AIS network (terrestrial and satellite receivers) through VesselFinder and refresh automatically. A ship outside receiver range shows its last received position and the time it was received, which is how AIS works everywhere; the map never invents a position. Click a vessel on the map for name, type, speed, course and destination.
