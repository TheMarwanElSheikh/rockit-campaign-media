## 1. Drewry WCI falls 3% to $4,176/FEU, but Shanghai–Genoa jumps 6% to $4,436
**Summary:** Drewry's World Container Index (WCI) composite fell 3% to $4,176 per 40ft container in its assessment for Thursday 19 November 2026, the fourth weekly fall in a row. The fall came from the transpacific. Shanghai–New York fell 9% to $8,194 (the lowest in 15 weeks) and Shanghai–Los Angeles fell 5% to $7,026 (the lowest in 12 weeks). On Asia–Europe/Med, Shanghai–Genoa rose 6% to $4,436 after three flat weeks, while Shanghai–Rotterdam held at $3,496. Drewry says many carriers have announced PSS of $1,000–1,600 per 40ft on Asia–Europe/Med from December, and it expects Asia–Europe rates to rise in the coming weeks while transpacific rates fall further. The WCI is still 126% higher year on year.

The decision followed a further assessment of Red Sea security. The first westbound sailings are *Mary Maersk* 650W from Ningbo on about 4 December 2026 (NE1), *Alette Maersk* 652W from Shanghai on about 20 December 2026 (NE3) and *Damietta Express* 702W from Shanghai on about 14 January 2027 (NE2). ## 10. IMO: ISWG-GHG 23–27 Nov, MEPC 85 30 Nov–3 Dec, Net-Zero Framework adoption session pencilled for 4 Dec

IMO's meeting calendar lists the Intersessional Working Group on GHG (ISWG-GHG) for 23–27 November, MEPC 85 for 30 November–3 December, and the resumed extraordinary session MEPC.ES/2 on 4 December "[subject to confirmation by MEPC 85]". That extraordinary session adjourned in October 2025 without adopting the Net-Zero Framework (NZF). On 18 November nine industry associations, including BIMCO, ICS, INTERTANKO, INTERCARGO and WSC, issued a joint call for a global deal. Their statement stops short of endorsing the NZF as drafted. She put the cost at $53–265bn a year, while backing further talks, with legal text "hoped for in early 2027". On 19 November IMO published a climate-finance study that found only $10.5bn of direct maritime climate finance in 2018–2023, 0.14% of global climate finance.
