## 1. Cabinet lifts export rebates 35% and adds freight support up to 50%

**Summary.** On Thursday 5 November the Cabinet approved a revamped export rebate programme. Support rises by an average of 35%, and the new rates apply to shipments from the start of November. Freezone exporters are excluded. A dedicated freight line covers:
- 50% of sea and land freight to Africa, Latin America, Australia, Southeast Asia and Russia
- 25% of air freight to Europe and 50% to the UK, for agricultural exports only
- 25% of domestic transport costs, 25% of cargo insurance policies and 25% of the invoice for shipments on express vessels

The US and 11 Southeast Asian countries join the target-market incentive list. Exporters must now submit complete files before they are accepted. Phase 1 runs to the end of 2026 and phase 2 starts in July 2027. On 15 November the Finance and Investment ministries were due to brief exporters at 4pm at the St. Regis in the New Administrative Capital.

**Key figures**
- Average rebate increase: +35%. The increase varies by sector.
- Freight support: 50% sea/land to new markets; 25% air to Europe; 50% air to UK (agri); 25% inland transport, 25% insurance, 25% express-vessel invoice.
- FY 2026/27 budget for export support: EGP 48 bn, against EGP 28 bn actually disbursed last fiscal year.
- FY 2025/26 non-oil exports: USD 37.3 bn (+7.8%). Trade deficit: USD 64.8 bn (+27.1%).
- Sectors covered: textiles, agri/food, engineering, medical/pharma, furniture, leather, chemicals and packaging, building materials, handicrafts.

**Date:** Cabinet approval Thu 5 Nov 2026. Reported 8 Nov. Exporter briefing Sun 15 Nov.

## 14. Gulf and Africa: Hormuz bypass trucking, AD Ports record, GCC rail
