This is the full edition of The Rockit Manifest, the weekly review we publish every Monday for exporters, importers, manufacturers and fellow forwarders who move cargo through Egypt.

Every figure is footnoted to the page we read it on. Where we could not verify a claim, we left it out; where a claim comes from one side of a conflict, we say so. The analysis lines marked Why it matters are ours, and they are kept apart from the reporting. — The Rockit desk, Cairo

Rockit's read

Three moves for this week

  • Importing from Asia? Book December cargo against index-linked or negotiated rates, not the $6,500–6,600 tariff, and ask for an all-in quote that lists every surcharge.
  • Exporting? Check whether your markets are on the 50% freight-support list, and build complete rebate files from the first shipment: incomplete files are now refused.
  • Shipping to the Gulf or by air? Confirm the routing (Gulf bypass ports or Saudi Red Sea ports) and lock air space before the late-November peak.

Ocean freight

Rates · December tariffs · surcharges
Lead · Asia → Mediterranean

The index says down. The tariff says up.

Three independent indices put Asia–Med spot between $4,168 and $4,666 a box. December's published tariffs to Egypt start at $6,500.

Spot container rates eased again last week. Drewry's World Container Index fell 2% to $4,305 per 40ft on 12 November. Shanghai–Rotterdam slipped 3% to $3,504, while Shanghai–Genoa, Drewry's closest proxy for Egypt's Mediterranean ports, held at $4,168. Freightos put Asia–Mediterranean at $4,300 (−1%), and Xeneta's Far East–Med average was $4,666 (−0.9%) on 13 November.

Carriers are pricing December differently. Hapag-Lloyd's Far East–Europe tariff from 1 December sets Türkiye, Egypt and the Eastern Mediterranean at $5,300 per 20ft and $6,600 per 40ft. MSC's December rate lifts East Med 40ft boxes by $800 to $6,500, valid to 14 December at the latest. From 15 November MSC also adds a combined $91 per TEU piracy-risk and Suez Canal surcharge on Asian cargo to the remaining Mediterranean, North African and Black Sea ports.

Drewry counts eight blank sailings on Asia–Europe/Med next week, up from five, and still expects those rates to “increase slightly in the coming weeks”. Xeneta's chief analyst Peter Sand is less convinced: “gravity always wins”.3,14,15,24,27,28

Why it mattersA published tariff is a carrier's opening position, not the market. With the gap at roughly $1,800–2,400 per 40ft, Asia–Egypt importers have room to negotiate below December's tariffs, but only with an all-in quote that names every surcharge.

Suez & Hormuz

Two straits · services · security
Suez Canal

The canal's comeback, service by service

Every major alliance now has at least one service through Suez again. Most North Europe loops are still on the Cape.

Evergreen's 16,556 TEU Ever Eon transited northbound on 8 November, the carrier's first Suez passage since it suspended Red Sea routings in December 2023. A week later, on 15 November, ONE's 8,110 TEU ONE Continuity crossed southbound on the Premier Alliance FE1 loop. Suez Canal Authority chairman Ossama Rabiee said three more Premier Alliance services will switch from the Cape to the canal; Freightos expects Yang Ming in December and full alliance returns by Q1 2027.

Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd moved their joint ME1 India–Europe service to Suez in early November. Hapag-Lloyd now routes five Far East–South Europe services, one Far East–North Europe service and two India–Europe services through the canal; its other North Europe services still sail via the Cape.

The money is following. Canal revenue reached $724m in October, up 94% year on year and the highest since the Red Sea crisis began, on transits up 32% to 1.5k. Xeneta counts 467,297 TEU of weekly capacity committed to the Red Sea in October: half the pre-crisis level, and roughly double the end-of-August figure.1,6,7,9,12,31

Why it mattersThe services recovering first are the ones that pass Egypt: Asia–South Europe and India–Europe. Check each booking's routing (Suez or Cape) rather than assume it.

Flexport, quoted by The Loadstar, warns that ships arriving via Suez and the Cape together could push North Europe arriving capacity to 131% of normal in week 47.36

The other strait: Hormuz

UKMTO's latest weekly count, reported on 13 November, was 139 outbound and 169 inbound Hormuz transits, with traffic about 65% below pre-conflict levels and eight incidents, seven of them tanker attacks. The Joint Maritime Information Center has logged 181 incidents since 1 March and about 211 ships waiting at anchor east of the strait.2,11

Rockit explains · in one paragraph

Why spot and tariff diverge in a reopening

When ships return to a shorter route, the same fleet can make more voyages, so effective capacity rises before demand does. Spot rates, set by cargo chasing space week by week, react first. Tariffs and surcharges, set by carriers trying to protect revenue, react last. Xeneta estimates the Suez return has already released 599k TEU of capacity and forecasts spot rates 30% lower in 2027. The gap between index and tariff is the negotiating room.9

Air cargo

Peak season · rates · capacity
Air freight

A peak season priced like one

Rates are at their 2026 high, fuel has doubled, and Gulf capacity is still 18% short of pre-war.

The Baltic Air Freight Index rose 5.3% in the four weeks to 2 November, leaving it 22.8% above last year and above the highest points of the previous two peak seasons, TAC Index says. In the week to 9 November it rose another 6.6% to its highest level of 2026, with “chunky gains” from Europe on routes including to the UAE.

IATA's September data show demand (CTK) up 7.7% against capacity up 2.7%, lifting the global load factor to 47.9%. Middle Eastern carriers had the weakest demand growth, +3.8%, while adding 7.0% capacity; Europe–Middle East traffic fell 18.0%. Jet fuel was 103.4% above a year earlier.

WorldACD's October figures put Africa-origin spot rates at $3.11/kg, up 60% year on year, as tight capacity and fuel surcharges met the start of the perishables export season. Capacity to and from Arabian Gulf markets was still 18% below its pre-war level. Xeneta sees few signs of a further Christmas surge and expects some cargo to shift back to ocean from Q2 2027.4,16,26,45,46

Why it mattersEgyptian perishables and urgent-parts shippers should book capacity now and budget for fuel surcharges well above last year. Express heavyweight services are a fallback when general cargo space runs out.

What the forwarder results mean for you

The big forwarders are earning more from air freight by choosing which cargo to carry: tech and hyperscaler freight gets space first, and DSV says it is trading volume for yield. Smaller shippers should hold block-space or allocation agreements, or work through a forwarder that does. Consolidation among mid-tier forwarders also means agency networks will change; check who your partner's partner is.

Europe's de minimis ends, parcels drop

Since the EU's €3 duty on parcels under €150 began on 1 July, Asia–Europe e-commerce air volumes fell 61% year on year in Q3 (Xeneta). WorldACD reports a further €2-per-item EU handling fee from 1 November, expected to cut volumes further.16,46

Geopolitics & trade policy

The 9 January cliff
Washington & Beijing

Every deadline now lands on 9 January

Both sides of the US–China truce, and the US port fees on Chinese ships, expire within a day of each other.

The US Trade Representative suspended its Section 301 port fees on Chinese-built and Chinese-operated ships for two more months, to 11:59pm ET on 9 January 2027 (91 FR 72608, published 10 November). The same day it extended all 178 remaining product exclusions in the main Section 301 China tariff case to the same date.

China mirrored the move. Its 24% additional tariff on US goods stays suspended for two more months (a 10% tariff remains), and its port fees on US-linked ships and critical-mineral export waivers run to 10 January. Reuters reported that Xi and Trump were expected to meet at the APEC summit in Shenzhen on 18–19 November.

The tariffs struck down in February stay in the news. After the Supreme Court ruled the IEEPA tariffs unlawful, UPS has gone to the Court of International Trade to force refunds on finally liquidated entries; The Loadstar reports UPS has received about $500m and still owes customers $270m. China's commerce ministry noted that Washington intends to use Section 301 tariffs to replace the IEEPA duties. On Section 232, the White House chose 180 days of talks rather than tariffs on robotics and industrial machinery.19,20,57,58,59,60,61

Why it mattersUS-lane quotes that run past 9 January need a surcharge clause. Egyptian exporters who shipped DDP to the US should check who was importer of record: refund rights follow that name.

The IMO Council (2–6 Nov) condemned Houthi attacks in the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab and Russian attacks on Ukraine's Black Sea corridor. Net-Zero Framework returns

The IMO set MEPC 85 for 30 Nov–3 Dec. gCaptain reported in September that the framework could return for adoption at an extraordinary session on 4 December. If adopted, it enters into force 16 months later for ships over 5,000 GT.62,70,71

Egypt desk

Rebates · money · ports · industry
Exports

Cairo will pay half the freight, if the file is complete

A revamped rebate programme gives freight its own support line, and refuses incomplete files.

The Cabinet approved the revamped export rebate programme on 5 November. Support rises by an average of 35%, and the new rates already apply to shipments from the start of November; free-zone exporters are excluded. For the first time, freight has its own line: the state covers 50% of sea and land freight to Africa, Latin America, Australia, Southeast Asia and Russia, and for agricultural exports 25% of air freight to Europe and 50% to the UK.

The programme also pays 25% of domestic transport, of cargo insurance and of express-vessel invoices. The US and 11 Southeast Asian countries join the target-market list. Exporters must submit complete files before they are accepted. Phase one runs to the end of 2026 and phase two starts in July 2027. The FY 2026/27 budget earmarks EGP 48bn for export support, against EGP 28bn actually paid out last year.21

Why it mattersFreight invoices, B/Ls and insurance certificates are now part of the exporter's cash flow. Ask your forwarder for claim-ready documents from the first shipment.

Egypt & the region

Industry · FX · ports · neighbours
FX outlook

Banks split on the pound

Deutsche Bank sees the pound at 48 per dollar if the US–Iran war ends and beyond 60 if it escalates, and expects the CBE to hold rates. It says it has not heard of “significant import delays” from FX shortages. Remittances hit a record $38.2bn in January–September.5,73

Why it mattersFreight is billed in dollars. Watch quote validity and FX clauses.
Industry

Gas rationing deepens

Gas cuts for fertiliser and methanol plants rose to 33%, and Ezz Steel was notified of a 30% cut, per Asharq Business (disputed by an industry source). Five of 15 November LNG cargoes were deferred, and urea is fetching about $535/t.76

Why it mattersExpect lumpier urea, ammonia and methanol export volumes; confirm cargo readiness.
SCZone

Sokhna gets a digital truck yard

SCZone hired Telecom Egypt to upgrade networks at East Port Said and Sokhna. A Nafeza-run digital truck yard at Sokhna will handle up to 1.1k trucks a day, and Sailun's $804m tyre plant rolled out its first tyre.77

EU

CBAM bill: about $317m a year

Egypt is asking Brussels for a phased CBAM timetable. Environment Ministry studies put the cost of non-compliance at about $317m a year, of which steel accounts for $236m. Separately, the EU plans to recognise Egyptian e-signatures, a first outside Europe.66,78

Gulf

Around Hormuz by truck

DP World has moved more than 1m TEU overland from UAE east-coast ports since March, about 3.5k TEU a day. AD Ports posted record Q3 net income of AED 975m, while UAE container throughput fell 57%. GCC states signed the framework for a 2.1k-km railway.79,80,81

Why it mattersQuote Gulf cargo via Khorfakkan, Fujairah or Sohar plus trucking, or via Saudi Red Sea ports.
State finances

A gate on public FX spending

The Cabinet approved three months of spending rationalisation: any state spending with a foreign-currency component now needs Finance Ministry approval, coordinated with the CBE.21

Rail

Sinai freight line gathers pace

Egypt is accelerating construction of a 341km railway linking Bir al-Abd, Arish and Taba, Daily News Egypt reports.82

Energy routes

Iraqi crude looks for Red Sea and Med outlets

Iraqi, Egyptian and Jordanian officials discussed new Red Sea and Mediterranean outlets for Iraqi crude, including the $4.6bn Basra–Haditha pipeline (about 2.3m barrels a day). No terminals or timetable were named.83

United States

Cairo takes US trade barriers to the USTR

Investment and Foreign Trade Minister Mohamed Farid met US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer in Washington to review the economic partnership and discuss bilateral trade flows.84

Africa & Europe

New doors open

Sudan lifted its import ban on 44 of 46 goods and zeroed duties on staples. The PM proposed extending the Alexandria–Koper port partnership to Sokhna and Damietta. DP World starts a weekly China–East Africa link in late November.85,86,87

Rockit explains

Anatomy of an Asia → Egypt ocean quote

Spot indices, published tariffs and the number on your invoice are three different things. In December they are far apart, so it pays to know which line is which.

LineWhat it isWhat to ask
Base freightFAK tariff, or a negotiated / index-linked rateIs this tariff, spot or contract? Which index is it linked to?
FuelBAF or MFR: bunker cost recoveryIs fuel included in the base? Which month's fuel price?
SurchargesPeak season (PSS), piracy-risk, Suez Canal, security, EU ETS on EU legsList every surcharge and its effective date
RoutingSuez or Cape of Good HopeWhich way does this service sail? Transit days?
Local chargesTHC, documentation, free time at Alexandria, Damietta, Port Said or SokhnaFree days for demurrage and detention?
ValidityThe date the price stops holding (MSC's December FAK: 14 Dec at the latest)Valid until which date: booking, loading or sailing?
If you import
  • Benchmark every Asia quote against $4,168–4,666 per 40ft spot (Drewry, Freightos, Xeneta).
  • Budget $91 per TEU for MSC's piracy and Suez surcharges from 15 November.
  • File ACI data early: firms report slower customs procedures in October's PMI.
  • Price FX risk: the pound moved 3 points in three weeks.
If you export
  • Check if your market is on the 50% freight-support list: Africa, Latin America, SE Asia, Australia, Russia.
  • Keep freight, insurance and inland invoices claim-ready; incomplete files are refused.
  • Perishables by air: Africa-origin spot is up 60%; book capacity early.
  • Steel, aluminium, fertiliser or cement to the EU: prepare emissions data for CBAM.
If you forward
  • Quote December with hard validity, and state the routing on every booking.
  • Add a 10 January clause to US-lane quotes (port-fee pause ends 9 Jan).
  • Quote Gulf cargo via bypass ports or Saudi Red Sea gateways.
  • Screen Russia-linked cargo against US, EU and UK lists separately.

Glossary
FAK · Freight All Kinds: a carrier's published base rate for general cargo, regardless of commodity.
GRI · General Rate Increase: an announced across-the-board rate rise; not always achieved in the market.
PSS · Peak Season Surcharge: an add-on charged in busy periods.
BAF / MFR · Bunker Adjustment Factor / Marine Fuel Recovery: the fuel component of a sea freight rate.
TEU / FEU · Twenty- and forty-foot equivalent units: one 40ft box is two TEU.
WCI / FBX · Drewry World Container Index and Freightos Baltic Index: weekly spot-rate benchmarks.
CTK / ACTK · Cargo tonne-kilometres (demand) and available cargo tonne-kilometres (capacity) in air freight.
BAI00 · Baltic Air Freight Index, global: TAC Index's air rate benchmark.
Blank sailing · A scheduled voyage a carrier cancels to cut capacity.
De minimis · The value below which parcels enter a country duty-free; the EU removed it on 1 July 2026.
Section 301 · US trade law used for tariffs and port fees on China.
CBAM · EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism: a carbon price on imports of steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser and more.
EU ETS · EU Emissions Trading System, which now covers shipping emissions.
ACI / ACID · Egypt's Advance Cargo Information system and the 19-digit ACID number every import needs before shipment.
