# Fact-check ledger: The Rockit Manifest, Voyage 001, full edition (16 Nov 2026)

Every figure in the 16-page edition comes from a page fetched and read between 9 and 16 Nov 2026. The four desk reports in `research/` record, for each item, the URL, the publication date, verbatim quotes and a status (VERIFIED or PARTIAL). The raw page copies were kept in the build session's scratch space.

## Rules applied
- No figure from a search snippet alone. Anything we could not read on a fetched page was dropped (see below).
- Claims by a party to a conflict are attributed. Examples: Iranian state media on the mine strikes, which Reuters could not verify; Russia's defence ministry on the Black Sea strikes; Asharq Business on the Ezz Steel cut, which an industry source disputes.
- Trade-press reports of carrier tariffs (Hapag-Lloyd, MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM) are labelled "as reported by Container News".
- Analysis is confined to the *Why it matters* and *Rockit's read* blocks.

## Items held at PARTIAL, and how the text handles them
- **IMO extraordinary session on 4 Dec:** gCaptain (22 Sep) only. Printed as "gCaptain reported in September… could return", and as "(reported)" in the calendar.
- **EU ETS 100% for 2026 emissions:** gCaptain (9 Oct), a secondary source. Printed as a gCaptain-sourced fact.
- **EU €2-per-item handling fee from 1 Nov:** WorldACD via STAT Times. Printed as "WorldACD reports".
- **Section 232 proclamation:** dates from the Federal Register API metadata; the details from Supply Chain Dive.
- **Maersk Logistics & Services figures:** FreightWaves only. Attributed in the text.
- **MSC East Med zone:** Container News does not say whether each Egyptian port falls in the zone. The ocean table tells readers to confirm with the carrier.

## Not printed (could not verify on an allowed host)

- SCA monthly vessel count of "1,490" (Egypt Today returned 403). We used EnterpriseAM's SCA statement instead: transits +32% to 1.5k.
- The "Talara" seizure in Hormuz on 14 Nov (appears to be a 2025 event).
- US EO 14436 (Russia sanctions waiver); Section 301 country rates for Egypt; Breakbulk ME, Intermodal Africa and TransMEA dates.
- A Durban congestion item (no saved page); COSCO, Evergreen and CEVA Q3 results.
