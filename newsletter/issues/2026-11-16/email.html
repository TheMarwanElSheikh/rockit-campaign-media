Line 01 · The straight answer

Suez is back. Your December rate isn't going down.

The ships are returning. On 8 November Evergreen's EVER EON transited the canal northbound, the line's first transit in nearly three years. The Suez Canal Authority now lists CMA CGM, Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd, COSCO and Evergreen back on Asia–Europe services.

The prices aren't following. Spot is drifting: Drewry's Shanghai–Genoa held at $4,168 per 40ft on 12 November. But MSC's Far East to East Med FAK goes to $6,500 from 1 December, and CMA CGM adds a $1,600 peak surcharge to the Med from 25 November. Drewry itself expects Asia–Europe/Med rates to rise slightly in the coming weeks.

The index is what the market averaged last week. The tariff is what your box is billed. This month they point in opposite directions.

Sources: Suez Canal Authority, 8 Nov · Drewry WCI, 12 Nov · MSC via Container News, 12 Nov · CMA CGM via Container News, 11 Nov
 
Line 02 · For exporters

Half the freight. Paid by Cairo.

Egypt's revamped export rebate programme, approved by the Cabinet on 5 November, raises support by an average of 35% on shipments made from the start of November. Free-zone exporters are excluded. This time freight gets its own line.

The catch: files must now be complete before they are accepted. The ministries briefed exporters on 15 November, and we'll bring you the practical rules next Monday. Until then, send us your target lanes and we'll line up the shipping documents your file will need.

Sources: EnterpriseAM, 8 Nov (Cabinet statement) · EnterpriseAM, 15 Nov

Line 04 · Egypt desk
MD1 is back on Suez

The Premier Alliance's MD1 has returned to the Suez routing. It calls Sokhna on the way in and Damietta in both directions, starting with YM Trust from Ningbo on 13 November. The round voyage takes 84 days with 12 ships, against 16 on the Cape.

Source: The Loadstar
The pound, off its low

The CBE's dollar sell rate stands at 53.50, against a record 56.35 in October. EnterpriseAM credits the 29 October rate hike with drawing foreign buyers back into local debt. The deposit rate is 20.0%, urban inflation eased to 12.7%, and the next rate decision is on 17 December.

Source: EnterpriseAM, 8 and 15 Nov
Signed in Cairo, valid in Brussels

Egypt is the first country outside Europe whose e-signature framework is technically aligned with the EU's. The European Commission plans to add Egypt to its trusted lists, so EU states can validate Egyptian e-signatures and seals, and Egypt will validate European ones. The EU accounts for 24.6% of Egypt's trade. Paperless trade documents just got a step closer.

Source: EnterpriseAM, 15 Nov (EU and ITIDA statements)
Clear before the delivery order

Under Decree 262/2026, a customs declaration can be opened before the D/O is issued, provided the D/O arrives before final release. "To Order" bills of lading are the exception. Send us documents with the pre-alert, not on arrival.

Source: EY Tax News; Al Tamimi & Co.
 
Line 05 · World desk
US port fees on Chinese ships: paused to 9 January

USTR extended the Section 301 suspension to 11:59 p.m. ET on 9 January 2027. The fee falls on ship operators, so for now there is nothing to pass on to cargo.

Source: gCaptain, 10 Nov
Air freight: full peak

The Baltic Air Freight Index rose 5.3% in the four weeks to 2 November and stands 22.8% above last year. Urgent year-end air? Confirm space this week.

Source: TAC Index, 5 Nov
Suez is open. Hormuz is not.

Drewry counted 53 Suez transits in the previous week against a single one through Hormuz. On 14 November Iranian state media reported a tanker hit a mine there, which Reuters could not immediately verify. For Gulf cargo, confirm routing before you promise a date.

Source: Drewry, 12 Nov; Reuters via gCaptain, 14 Nov
